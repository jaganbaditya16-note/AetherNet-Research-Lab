How to read the outcome

Our purpose is to find an architecture worth improving, not force the results to look good.

What would count as progress?

  • Better held-out accuracy at similar prediction time.
  • Comparable accuracy with measurably lower latency or memory.
  • A benefit that persists across seeds and real public datasets.
  • Ablations prove which new mechanism caused the benefit.

What this lab does not prove

  • It does not establish a first-ever model or patentable novelty.
  • Training and inference still use your host computer's physical resources.
  • Serialized model size is not peak RAM; timings are machine-dependent.
  • Synthetic data results do not establish general intelligence or lower energy.
